Openings

We are passionate about the design and engineering of materials & systems for energy harvesting, conversion, and storage applications, helping our society transition to a carbon-neutral future.

Our approaches are centered on understanding and fostering collaborative intelligence, where scientists' abilities are amplified by artificial intelligence and robotic maneuvers.

Latest Updates!

Shijing Sun has recently shared exciting lectures on how machine learning is transforming the way we think about energy and materials:

Smarter Batteries: Showing how data-driven methods can predict when a battery might fail and help extend its lifespan. This has big implications for energy storage and electric vehicles.

Closed-Loop Optimization: Demonstrating how AI can accelerate discovery and innovation by guiding the design of experiments. Applications range from materials discovery and engineering optimization to improving battery charging protocols.
