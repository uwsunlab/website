Advancing Thin-Film Fabrication with High-Throughput Robotic Automation

Puffin is an automated, high-throughput robotic platform developed by Sciprios, designed to streamline and control thin-film fabrication through an intuitive graphical user interface. This compact system integrates both blade coating and spin coating modules, enabling the execution of up to eight distinct synthesis experiments in a single cycle. In addition to its core deposition capabilities, Spinbot is equipped with multiple functional stations, including a substrate annealing unit, a photo station, and a vacuum quenching station supporting a fully automated step-by-step workflow for the treatment and fabrication of thin films.
