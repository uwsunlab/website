Andrew McDaniel

I am a part-time graduate student at UW pursuing a master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering. Before pursuing my master’s at UW, I completed two bachelor’s degrees. The first is in Applied Physics from UW, and the other is in Artificial Intelligence from Bellevue College. I also work as a Machine Learning Engineer at HeadLight, where I work on computer vision applications for construction management software. I share Sun Lab’s vision of accelerating the development of clean energy technologies using machine learning and look forward to making contributions to this area of research. Outside of work and studying, I like to go running, play video/board games, and read books.
