Our work is highly interdisciplinary, bringing together materials science, robotics, and artificial intelligence to address global energy and climate challenges.

Postdoctoral applicants with backgrounds in materials chemistry, lab automation, statistics, machine learning, or AI for clean energy are welcome to get in touch. Prospective Ph.D. students are encouraged to apply through the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge and contact Shijing to discuss research interests. Current University of Cambridge master's students are also welcome to explore research opportunities with the group.
