I received my BS degree in Materials Science from S.Petersburg Electrotechnical University (LETI) in 1986, a PhD in Electronics from the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences in May 1997, and an MSc in Hydrogen Safety Engineering from the University of Ulster in 2012. I joined the Department of Materials Science & Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge in 1999. My research interests focus on nano-engineered materials for energy generation and storage, solid-state electrochemical devices, and automated materials discovery.
