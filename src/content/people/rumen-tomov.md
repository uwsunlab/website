Rumen received his BS degree in Materials Science from St. Petersburg Electrotechnical University (LETI) in 1986, a PhD in Electronics from the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences in 1997, and an MSc in Hydrogen Safety Engineering from the University of Ulster in 2012. He joined the Department of Materials Science & Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge in 1999. His research interests focus on nano-engineered materials for energy generation and storage, solid-state electrochemical devices, and automated materials discovery.
