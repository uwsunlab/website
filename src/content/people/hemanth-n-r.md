Hemanth Neelgund Ramesh is a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Washington. His research focuses on developing AI agents and collaborative robotic systems for autonomous scientific discovery, with an emphasis on materials science. Previously, he has worked on batteries, electrolyzers, diffusion models and LLM-based scientific information extraction through collaborations with Amazon and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. He received his M.S. in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of Washington and his B.Tech. from NITK Surathkal.

Outside the lab, Hemanth enjoys hiking, swimming, cooking, and playing board games.
