Ghanshyam is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge, where he works on automated X-ray diffraction analysis workflows. He completed his PhD in the same department, working on polymer physics, machine learning-assisted polymer processing, and advanced materials characterisation, with a focus on recycled polymers and polymer-graphene nanocomposites. He previously worked as a visiting researcher and lecturer in Environmental Engineering at UCL, studying low-cost wastewater desalination. He holds an MRes in Graphene Technology from the Department of Engineering at Cambridge, an MSc in Renewable Energy Systems from UCL, and a BSc (Hons) in Chemistry and Biomedicine from King's College London.
