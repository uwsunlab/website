I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge working on X-ray diffraction analysis automation workflows. Prior to this, I completed my PhD within the same department in polymer physics, machine learning-assisted polymer processing, and advanced materials characterisation, with a focus on macromolecular properties of recycled polymers and polymer-graphene nanocomposites for high-performance purposes. I previously worked at a visiting researcher/lecturer at UCL in Environmental Engineering looking into low-cost wastewater desalination. I have a MRes degree in Graphene Technology from the Department of Engineering at Cambridge, an MSc degree in Renewable Energy Systems from UCL, and a BSc (hons) degree in Chemistry and Biomedicine from King’s College London. Outside of academia, my hobbies include sports (football and tennis), boardgame/movie nights, and trekking.
